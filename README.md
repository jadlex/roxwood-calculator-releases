# Roxwood Calculator — Downloads

Job calculator for Roxwood with accounts, clock-in/clock-out, leave and team reports.

## Download

Go to **[Latest release](https://github.com/jadlex/roxwood-calculator-releases/releases/latest)** and pick one:

- **`Roxwood-Calculator-Setup-x.y.z.exe`** – installer (recommended). Updates itself automatically.
- **`Roxwood-Calculator-Portable-x.y.z.exe`** – runs without installing.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: click **More info → Run anyway**.

## First use

Open the app, choose **Register**, and create your account with your name, email and a password.
